Projects

  • Two-Stage Multi-View DenseNet121 for Mammogram Classification

    Python, PyTorch, Medical Imaging

    • Built a two-stage multi-view DenseNet121 pipeline on VinDr-Mammo (4,750 studies, 9,499 paired CC/MLO breast samples) mirroring the radiologist workflow: Stage 1 screens normal (BI-RADS 1) vs. abnormal (2-6), Stage 2 separates benign (2-3) from suspicious or malignant findings (4-6).
    • Compared ResNet, DenseNet, VGG, and ViT; trained on a 50/50 undersampled set and tested on natural-distribution held-out sets (1,426 and 470 samples): Stage 1 sensitivity 62.6% (F1 0.563), Stage 2 sensitivity 50.7% (F1 0.455).
  • AI Maternal Health Assistant (AMHA)

    Google Gemini API, React, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Vercel

    • Designed, built, and deployed an end-to-end AI maternal health assistant supporting English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu, incorporating text-to-speech for pregnancy-related guidance; tested with 10 users across dozens of topics.
    • Addressed maternal health gaps in Pakistan (5M+ pregnancies annually, doctor-to-patient ratio of 1:1300).
  • Cell Analysis in R: From Gene Data to Machine Learning

    R, Seurat, caret, kNN

    • BIO312 course project: analyzed 10x Genomics PBMC 1k scRNA-seq data with Seurat (normalization, 2,000 highly variable genes, PCA, UMAP) and identified 8 cell clusters, assigning T-cell (CD3D), B-cell (MS4A1), and monocyte-like (VCAN) populations via marker genes.
    • Trained a kNN classifier (caret) on PCA features using a stratified 70/30 split, reaching 96.82% accuracy (kappa = 0.96) on 220 held-out cells.
