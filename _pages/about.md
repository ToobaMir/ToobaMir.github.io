I am a final-year BS Bioinformatics student at COMSATS University Islamabad (GPA 3.98/4.0, class rank 1), graduating in January 2027. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, bioinformatics, and healthcare, with a focus on building AI systems that clinicians and patients can trust.

As a Mitacs Globalink Research Intern at the [Flourish Lab](https://www.mcmaster.ca/) at McMaster University, I co-develop features for **Hey Mama!**, a clinical maternal and postpartum health app affiliated with Hamilton Health Sciences. I engineered its AI companion as a fully local, privacy-preserving retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline over 170 clinical guidelines, so answers stay grounded in the guidelines and no patient data leaves local infrastructure. I presented this work as an oral talk, _Beyond ChatGPT: A Clinical AI That Never Leaves the Building_, to medical students and physicians at McMaster Children's Hospital.

Before that, I led a team of four interns on breast cancer classification from mammograms at the National Center of Artificial Intelligence in Pakistan, and helped build **ChemAware**, a mobile health informatics app, where I supervised ten interns and curated a 10,000-sample labeled dataset.

I also enjoy teaching. I am currently a teaching assistant for _Introduction to Bioinformatics_ and _Programming for Artificial Intelligence_ at COMSATS, and I previously served as Technical Lead of Google Developer Groups on Campus, where I taught programming to 50+ students and made a YouTube playlist of C fundamentals in Urdu.

You can find my [publications](/publications/), [projects](/projects/), and [CV](/cv/) in the menu above.
