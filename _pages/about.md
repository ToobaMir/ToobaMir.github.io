I am a final-year BS Bioinformatics student at COMSATS University Islamabad (GPA 3.98/4.0, class rank 1), graduating in January 2027. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, bioinformatics, and healthcare, with a focus on building AI systems that clinicians and patients can trust.

As a Mitacs Globalink Research Intern at the Flourish Lab at McMaster University, I co-develop features for **Hey Mama!**, a clinical maternal and postpartum health app affiliated with Hamilton Health Sciences. I engineered its AI companion as a fully local, privacy-preserving retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline over 170 clinical guidelines, so answers stay grounded in the guidelines and no patient data leaves local infrastructure.

Before that, I led a team of four interns on breast cancer classification from mammograms at the National Center of Artificial Intelligence in Pakistan, and helped build **ChemAware**, a mobile health informatics app.

## Projects

**Two-Stage Multi-View DenseNet121 for Mammogram Classification** | Python, PyTorch, Medical Imaging
A two-stage pipeline on VinDr-Mammo (4,750 studies, 9,499 paired CC/MLO breast samples) that mirrors the radiologist workflow: Stage 1 screens normal vs. abnormal, Stage 2 separates benign from suspicious or malignant findings. Compared ResNet, DenseNet, VGG, and ViT. [GitHub](https://github.com/ToobaMir/Densenet-for-Mammogram-Classification)

**AI Maternal Health Assistant (AMHA)** | Google Gemini API, React, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Vercel
An end-to-end AI maternal health assistant supporting English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu, with text-to-speech for pregnancy-related guidance; tested with 10 users across dozens of topics. [GitHub](https://github.com/ToobaMir/Maternal-Health-Assistant)

**Cell Analysis in R: From Gene Data to Machine Learning** | R, Seurat, caret, kNN
BIO312 course project: analyzed 10x Genomics PBMC 1k scRNA-seq data with Seurat and identified 8 cell clusters, then trained a kNN classifier reaching 96.82% accuracy on 220 held-out cells. [GitHub](https://github.com/ToobaMir/R-Project)
