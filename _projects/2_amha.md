I designed, built, and deployed an end-to-end AI maternal health assistant supporting **English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu**, with text-to-speech for pregnancy-related guidance. It was tested with 10 users across dozens of topics.

It addresses maternal health gaps in Pakistan (5M+ pregnancies annually, doctor-to-patient ratio of 1:1300).

**Stack:** Google Gemini API, React, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Vercel.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/ToobaMir/Maternal-Health-Assistant)
